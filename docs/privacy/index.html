Privacy policy

What TishOS accesses, where information can go, and what happens when you turn a feature off.

This policy covers the native TishOS companion app and this website, maintained by ZachTish. The app is currently in internal TestFlight testing. Features vary by platform and build. The proposed FinanceKit connection is described separately and is not currently available.

The essentials

  • The companion processes an explicitly selected vault and prepares local views for Apple features.
  • It has no developer-hosted vault account, advertising, or third-party analytics SDK.
  • Optional iCloud Relay, your vault sync provider, and Apple system features have their own data boundaries.
  • Turning a feature off does not necessarily erase records already written to your vault or Apple Health.
  • Support messages and website visits have separate handling, explained below.

1. Scope and responsibility

The native companion connects a user-selected Obsidian vault to supported Apple features. It is distinct from Obsidian, individual TPS plugins, storage providers, and external services a plugin may use. This policy explains the companion's role; it does not replace the policies or settings of those other products.

The companion does not upload your vault, Health records, or financial records to a developer-operated server. The developer does not have a central copy of those records merely because you use TishOS. Information you deliberately send for support is an exception and is handled as described in section 10.

TishOS does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. The current companion has no third-party analytics or tracking SDK. Its privacy manifests declare no tracking and no collected data; those declarations do not mean there are no optional network transfers or data stored by Apple and your chosen providers.

TishOS works with information you authorize or provide. The following describes the main categories, rather than implying every feature is enabled on every device.

3. Widgets, notifications, Live Activities, and search

The app and its widget extension share a limited local display cache. Depending on the supported view, it can contain titles, sanitized note excerpts, calendar details, statistics, or daily macro summaries. Some preview data is prepared before you place or select a particular widget. Widgets read this cache; they do not independently fetch vault data from the network.

Notifications can include the reminder title, body, and timing supplied by the verified Controller schedule. Live Activities can show selected item or timer information. These surfaces may be visible on the Lock Screen or to someone using your device. Display redaction depends on Apple's system behavior and your settings; marking content as private is not a guarantee that a visible widget hides it.

Siri and Spotlight integration is optional and off by default. Authorizing metadata indexing and enabling readable previews are separate choices. Supported actions can open or read selected current content after validation. Apple operates Siri, Spotlight, Shortcuts, notifications, and other system services under its own settings and privacy policy.

4. Vault-file sync and optional iCloud Relay

Your vault's storage provider

A vault is a folder you select. If it is stored or synchronized through iCloud Drive, Obsidian Sync, Dropbox, or another provider, that provider can transfer files written there, including supported exports, AI request files, and portable preferences. The provider's account, encryption, backup, retention, and sharing settings apply. TishOS does not control the provider's independent copies or retention.

The companion's Relay connection

Relay is separately enabled and uses your Apple account's private CloudKit database to transfer supported, limited workspace projections between authorized devices. These can include selected note excerpts, calendar and summary information, bounded search content where configured, Live Activity context, and reminder display details. Channel information uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage.

The Relay projection is not a complete vault backup. File-access bookmarks, pairing credentials, unrestricted Markdown, and general command authority are not included. Each receiving device must independently authorize its local vault and applicable features. Apple operates the iCloud infrastructure; private-account storage is not a promise that the developer controls Apple's processing or that every data category has the same encryption guarantees.

The developer does not choose a geographic processing region for Apple's services, GitHub hosting, or your file provider. Their applicable policies and account arrangements govern where they process information.

5. Optional Apple Health integration

Apple Health integration is off by default. On supported devices, you independently choose nutrition export and activity import, then authorize selected categories through Apple's permission system. Enabling the general integration alone does not request every Health category.

  • Nutrition export: write supported vault food entries to Apple Health. Depending on your installed build and selections, categories include dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, total fat, saturated/monounsaturated/polyunsaturated fats, fiber, sugar, sodium, cholesterol, potassium, caffeine, and water.
  • Activity import: read current-day totals for steps, active energy, exercise time, walking/running distance, and flights climbed.

These data support the selected nutrition and activity features. They are not used for advertising, profiling, or data mining. The private synchronization ledger and activity snapshot stay in protected local storage excluded from app-managed cloud Relay and device backup. The ledger retains minimal ownership and revision information to manage TishOS-created Health entries and prevent duplicates.

A supported daily activity widget can have a separate local projection of today's steps and exercise minutes. It does not receive the private Health ledger or raw sample history and is not sent through Relay. Its visibility follows the system-surface considerations in section 3.

Some internal test builds offer separately confirmed output of daily activity summaries into ordinary vault files. The selected file provider may then upload those files, and configured workspace views may show their values. This test feature remains subject to public-distribution review; user consent is not presented as an exception to Apple's restrictions on storing personal health information in iCloud. The private Health ledger and direct Health sample history are not part of that export.

Internal Watch workout and capture features

Some internal builds also include a Watch workout journal and quick-capture drafts for notes, food, or cash expenses. Manually entered sets, timing, draft text, and any entered amount are stored in protected, backup-excluded local files and can transfer to the paired iPhone through Watch Connectivity. A cash or food draft is not automatically a completed finance or nutrition transaction.

Optional Watch HealthKit workout processing separately requests permission to save a workout. Sensor metrics are not included in the manual journal, its Watch-to-iPhone transfer, or its document export. The iPhone offers explicitly confirmed export of a readable document; the destination provider may synchronize that export. These features are under internal testing, with physical-device and distribution validation still required.

6. Optional Apple Intelligence requests

On supported devices and builds, an explicitly requested TPS AI Gateway text job can be processed with Apple's on-device model. TishOS reads the specifically addressed job from the authorized vault, validates it, and writes a structured result or failure back to that request file. It does not automatically send all notes to an AI model.

Prompts and results can remain in the vault queue and be synchronized by the vault provider. Completing a request does not itself promise immediate deletion of those files; the producing workflow and your file-management choices determine their later retention.

The current integration does not have approval for Apple's managed Private Cloud Compute access and does not promise that service. Other AI providers configured in Obsidian plugins are separate integrations; review their settings and policies before sending them content.

7. Planned Apple Wallet / FinanceKit connection

FinanceKit approval is being pursued. The connection is not implemented or available, and the current companion does not read Apple Wallet financial data.

The proposed feature would request your permission to read supported Apple Card, Savings, and Apple Cash account information, balances, and transactions. Its purpose would be personal budgeting, spending review, and net-worth tracking through account and transaction notes in your user-controlled vault.

The intended path is your authorized iPhone → an encrypted financial-data relay through your selected vault sync provider → your paired Controller device → account and transaction notes. This planned relay is distinct from the companion's existing CloudKit display Relay. The resulting Markdown notes would use the vault's normal storage, backup, and sharing settings; the proposed encrypted transport does not mean every resulting file is independently encrypted.

No payment initiation or developer-hosted financial account service is planned. The policy, permission disclosures, retention and deletion behavior, and implementation will be updated and validated before this connection becomes available. This description is a development plan, not an assertion of Apple approval or currently verified operation.

8. Retention and deletion

There is no single retention period for all information because your vault, device, Apple services, and support records are separate stores. The important distinctions are:

Local settings, display caches, and search indexes
Retained while needed for the enabled features. Unlinking or opting out clears or invalidates the applicable derived state. Cleanup failures can leave retry work or undisplayable cached bytes until removal succeeds. System-managed display and indexing updates may not be immediate.
The active CloudKit Relay projection
Disabling Relay or unlinking on the publishing device requests deletion of its active record. Disabling a receiver removes that receiver's imported state; it does not erase the source vault or another device's independent source data. A pending cloud deletion needs service availability and may complete later.
Apple Health entries and ownership information
Disabling synchronization stops future work but does not automatically erase previously written Health entries. A minimal local ownership ledger may remain after unlinking to manage entries and prevent duplicate exports. Manage permissions and TishOS-created records through Apple Health. The app does not delete another source's Health entries.
Vault files, exports, and AI queue files
These remain ordinary files until you or the responsible workflow removes them. Unlinking TishOS or uninstalling it does not delete your vault, cloud copies, file history, or backups. Manage them through your vault and chosen provider.
Watch workout journals and capture drafts
Local journals, draft inboxes, and pending transfer records are independent of vault linking. Disconnecting a vault does not delete them, an exported document, or a workout already saved to Apple Health. Manage any exported or Health records in their destination; do not assume clearing one device clears the paired device.
Support and testing feedback
Voluntarily supplied messages can be retained to resolve problems and maintain a support record. GitHub issues may remain public in repository history; Apple controls TestFlight feedback retention. Removing a visible post may not remove historical, cached, or provider-held copies.

Deleting the app is not a complete erasure request for independent stores. It does not guarantee removal of Keychain items, shared extension data, iCloud records, Apple Health entries, or backups. Use the app's relevant disconnect controls before uninstalling where possible, and manage remaining copies in the service that owns them.

9. Security and practical limits

TishOS uses platform folder authorization, sandboxing, protected local storage where applicable, device-local credential storage, and validation of supported routes and paired messages. Credentials are not ordinary widget display fields. Supported handoffs recheck the current vault and destination rather than treating a link alone as unrestricted authority.

These measures do not make every vault file or displayed field secret. A sensitive value written into a normal title, excerpt, or property may appear in a configured view. Someone with access to your unlocked device, shared vault, backup, or authorized storage account may see information there. Choose permissions, widget placements, notification previews, and vault sharing accordingly.

Network services and backups use the protections supplied by their operators and your configuration. This policy makes no blanket end-to-end-encryption claim for all TishOS data and no guarantee of absolute security.

10. Website, support, and diagnostics

This website

The site has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, cookies, or browser storage of its own. GitHub Pages hosts the public files and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub's privacy statement explains its hosting and account processing. Following a link to another website subjects that visit to the destination's practices.

Voluntary support messages

Messages submitted through GitHub issues include your GitHub identity and the text or attachments you choose to provide. They are used for responding, troubleshooting, and maintaining the project. Issues are public. Do not post health or financial records, private notes, credentials, or screenshots containing personal information. Describe the problem in general terms first.

App diagnostics and TestFlight

The companion's local diagnostic logging is designed to use bounded counts, stages, and outcomes rather than note bodies, credentials, or full settings. Logs are not automatically sent to a developer analytics service. If you choose to share a diagnostic file, review it first; surrounding operating-system or plugin logs may have different content.

Apple may separately process installation, crash, diagnostic, and feedback information for TestFlight or App Store services. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit through TestFlight can be available to the developer. That processing is governed by Apple's policies and your applicable settings.

11. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose a different vault or unlink it, disable optional connections and system surfaces, change Apple permissions in system settings, remove Health records through Apple Health, and manage vault files through your storage provider. Turning off one feature does not automatically turn off other independently enabled features.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may provide rights relating to information held about you. For questions about information you provided directly to the developer, start with a non-sensitive request through the contact route below. Existing internal testers can also use TestFlight feedback. We do not need your vault, bank credentials, or complete Health history to receive a privacy question.

The developer cannot retrieve or erase your local vault or private Apple account data on your behalf. Requests concerning a provider's independently held information need to go to that provider. A public support comment is not a private document-submission channel.
