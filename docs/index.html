TishOS brings selected information from your Obsidian vault to widgets, reminders, and everyday actions on your Apple devices.

In development · Internal TestFlight testing

01

See what matters

View selected notes, calendars, and daily summaries through native widgets. Use reminders and Live Activities for the information you choose.

02

Work with your own vault

Connect a vault you control. Open its notes and supported TPS actions from the app, widgets, and Shortcuts.

03

Choose your connections

Optional Apple Health integration and cross-device iCloud Relay have separate controls. Permissions and supported features depend on your device and installed build.

Planned integration

Apple Wallet for personal finance

We are pursuing FinanceKit approval to let an authorized iPhone read Apple Card, Savings, and Apple Cash accounts, balances, and transactions. The planned connection will send selected data to your paired Controller device for account and transaction notes used in budgeting and net-worth tracking.

This connection is not available yet. Apple approval, implementation, and device testing are still required. It is intended for reading financial information, not initiating payments.
